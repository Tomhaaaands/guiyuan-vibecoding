# R16 · product+architecture（2026-08-31）

## Background

The product goal was clarified: VibeCoding_Manager is not a project-management system that makes
the user operate a backlog. It is the manager — an autonomous vibecoding R&D butler that accepts
natural-language intent, maintains product/design/technical truth, plans and executes work,
verifies delivery, prevents drift, and learns from outcome evidence. Private_butler remains a
low-coupling personal-context provider rather than the owner of project facts.
